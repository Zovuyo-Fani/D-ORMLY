About Dormly

Connecting students with safe, affordable and trusted accommodation.

Who We Are

Dormly is a student accommodation platform designed to help university students find quality housing quickly and safely. Our goal is to simplify the accommodation search process by connecting students with verified landlords and residences near campuses across South Africa.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading student accommodation marketplace by providing a trusted platform where students can confidently search, compare and book their next home.

Our Mission

We aim to make student living easier by offering affordable accommodation, transparent information, verified listings and a simple booking experience that saves time and reduces stress.

Why Choose Dormly?

🔒 Safe

Every listing is carefully reviewed to help students find secure accommodation.

💰 Affordable

Compare prices and choose accommodation that fits your budget.

📍 Convenient

Find residences close to universities and public transport.
