Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students need to know about Dormly

How do I book accommodation?

Go to the Listings page, choose a property you like, click "View Property", then proceed to booking and payment.

Is Dormly free to use?

Yes, browsing and creating an account is completely free. You only pay when booking accommodation.

Are the accommodations verified?

Yes, all listings are checked to ensure safety, quality, and legitimacy before being published.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, cancellations are allowed depending on the landlord’s policy. Check your booking details for terms.

How do I contact support?

You can use the Contact page or email support@dormly.co.za for assistance.

Can landlords list their properties?

Yes, landlords can register and upload properties through the landlord portal (coming soon in your system).
